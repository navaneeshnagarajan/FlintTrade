# Technical Indicators — Usage Guide

## RSI (Relative Strength Index, period 14)

**Overbought/Oversold:**
- RSI > 70 → overbought, watch for reversal (but can stay >70 in a strong trend)
- RSI < 30 → oversold, watch for bounce
- RSI 40–60 → neutral zone

**Divergence (more reliable than thresholds):**
- Bullish divergence: price makes lower low but RSI makes higher low → potential reversal up
- Bearish divergence: price makes higher high but RSI makes lower high → potential reversal down

**In trending markets:** Use RSI 40 as support in an uptrend; 60 as resistance in a downtrend.

## MACD (12, 26, 9)

**Signal line crossover:**
- MACD line crosses above signal line → bullish
- MACD line crosses below signal line → bearish

**Zero line crossover (stronger signal):**
- MACD crosses above zero → trend turns bullish
- MACD crosses below zero → trend turns bearish

**Histogram:** Rising histogram → momentum increasing; shrinking → momentum fading.

## Bollinger Bands (20, 2σ)

- Price near upper band in an uptrend → continuation signal
- Price near lower band in a downtrend → continuation signal
- Band squeeze (narrow bands) → breakout imminent, direction unknown — wait for confirmation
- Price touching upper band then reversing inside → mean reversion entry

## VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)

- Price above VWAP → bullish intraday bias; buy on dips to VWAP
- Price below VWAP → bearish intraday bias; sell on rallies to VWAP
- VWAP acts as dynamic support/resistance throughout the day
- Institutional benchmark: large orders anchored around VWAP

## Supertrend (ATR 7, multiplier 3)

- Green line below price → uptrend, stay long
- Red line above price → downtrend, stay short
- Flip in Supertrend direction → trend change signal
- Best used on 15m+ charts to filter noise

## EMA (9, 20, 50, 200)

- Price > 200 EMA → structural uptrend
- 9 EMA > 20 EMA > 50 EMA → strong uptrend alignment
- 9/20 EMA crossover → short-term entry trigger within the trend direction

## Combining Indicators

Recommended combination (avoid redundancy):
1. Trend direction: EMA 50/200 or Supertrend
2. Momentum: RSI or MACD
3. Entry timing: VWAP retest or Bollinger Band touch

Never combine RSI + Stochastic (both oscillators, same information).
Never combine MACD + RSI alone — add a trend filter.
