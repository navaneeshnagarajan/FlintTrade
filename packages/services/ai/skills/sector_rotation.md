# Sector Rotation

## What Is Sector Rotation?

Capital cycles between sectors as macro conditions change. Identifying which sectors are receiving inflows — and which are being sold — gives an edge in both stock selection and index positioning.

## Relative Strength Scoring

Relative Strength (RS) compares a sector's performance against a benchmark (Nifty 50):

```
RS Score = Sector % Change (N days) − Nifty % Change (N days)
```

Use multiple lookback periods (20d, 60d, 120d) and average the scores. A sector consistently above zero is in a leadership phase.

Key NSE sector indices: NIFTY IT, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY AUTO, NIFTY PHARMA, NIFTY FMCG, NIFTY METAL, NIFTY REALTY, NIFTY ENERGY.

## Momentum Scoring

Rank sectors by 3-month and 6-month price momentum. Enter the top 2–3 sectors, exit the bottom 2–3.

- Rebalance monthly or at each 20-day rebalance window.
- Combine with RS — only trade sectors that are both high-momentum AND showing positive RS.

## Correlation Breakdown as an Early Signal

When a sector's correlation with the Nifty drops sharply (from > 0.8 to < 0.5), it signals idiosyncratic sector news or institutional positioning. This often precedes a breakout or breakdown from the broader index trend.

Monitor rolling 20-day correlation between each sector index and Nifty 50.

## Intermarket Analysis

Global macro factors that reliably rotate Indian sector leadership:

| Factor | Rising Trend Favours | Falling Trend Favours |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| US 10Y Yield | PSU Banks, Financials | IT, FMCG |
| Crude Oil (Brent) | OMCs (if admin-priced), Metal | Paints, Airlines, FMCG |
| INR/USD (Rupee weakening) | IT exporters, Pharma | Importers (Metal, Crude consumers) |
| FII Flows (net buy) | Largecap leaders, Index | Defensive / Mid-small |
| DXY (US Dollar Index) | Pharma, IT | Metal, Commodities |

Cross-reference two or more intermarket signals before making a sector call to reduce false signals.
