# India Macro Indicators for Market Analysis

## RBI Monetary Policy

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meets 6 times per year (approximately every 2 months). The repo rate is the key lever.

| Rate Action | Typical Market Reaction |
|-------------|------------------------|
| Rate cut | Bullish equities, especially rate-sensitive sectors (banks, NBFCs, real estate) |
| Rate hike | Bearish short-term; banking NIM impact; rupee strengthens |
| Pause with dovish commentary | Mild bullish — markets price in future cuts |
| Stance change (neutral → accommodative) | Strong bullish signal, can trigger 1–2% Nifty rally |

**Trading rule:** Nifty tends to be volatile ±1% on RBI policy day. Sell straddles the session before (IV expansion into event); close immediately after announcement.

Key MPC dates announced quarterly — mark them in the event calendar.

## GDP, CPI, and IIP Data

**GDP Growth Rate (quarterly):**
- Released by MoSPI approximately 60 days after quarter end
- Below 6% → concerns about slowdown → sector rotation away from cyclicals
- Above 7% → risk-on, mid/small caps outperform
- India's long-term nominal GDP growth of 10–12% supports 12–14% Nifty earnings CAGR

**CPI Inflation (monthly, released 12th of following month):**
- RBI's target band: 2–6%, medium-term target 4%
- CPI above 6% → rate hike risk → negative for equity multiples
- CPI below 4% → opens room for cuts → positive for rate-sensitive sectors
- Food inflation (weight ~46% in CPI basket) is the most volatile component; watch it separately

**IIP (Index of Industrial Production, monthly):**
- Released with ~6-week lag
- Core IIP (cement, steel, electricity) drives market sentiment more than headline IIP
- IIP above 5% → manufacturing activity healthy → positive for capital goods, metals

## Monsoon Impact on NIFTY

India's agricultural sector is 14% of GDP but influences rural demand (≈40% of FMCG revenue) and food inflation.

| Monsoon Outcome | Market Impact |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Normal (96–104% of LPA) | Neutral to mildly positive |
| Excess (>104% LPA) | Mildly positive; watch for flood-related disruptions |
| Deficient (<90% LPA) | Negative: food inflation risk, rural demand fall |

**Timing:** IMD issues seasonal forecast in April (preliminary) and May (updated). Below-normal forecasts trigger defensive rotation (FMCG defensive vs. consumer discretionary sell).

Direct plays: Kaveri Seeds, Rallis India, UPL (agri inputs) benefit from good monsoon; Britannia, Nestlé benefit from lower input costs when monsoon controls food prices.

## Election Cycle Patterns

Indian equity markets show a consistent pattern around general elections (every 5 years):

- **12–6 months before election:** Fiscal stimulus, infra spending announcements → infrastructure, defence sectors outperform
- **2–3 months before election:** Volatility increases — India VIX spikes 20–40%; uncertainty premium
- **Election result day:** Nifty can move ±5–8% on counting day; buy straddles/strangles 5 days before
- **Post-election (stable government):** 6–12 month bull phase historically
- **State elections:** Watch for leading indicators of national sentiment; Karnataka/Maharashtra/Rajasthan results are proxies

## FPI (Foreign Portfolio Investor) Flow Impact

FPIs hold ≈18–20% of NSE market cap. Their flows have outsized impact on large caps.

- **Net FPI buy > ₹5,000 Cr/week:** Broad market tailwind; Nifty tends to rise
- **Net FPI sell > ₹5,000 Cr/week:** Selling pressure; mid/small caps hit harder
- Check NSE FPI data daily — available on NSE website and via screener APIs
- FPI selling in debt markets (G-Secs) often precedes equity selling by 2–3 sessions — a leading indicator

**Trigger monitoring:** US Fed decisions, dollar index moves, and EM risk-off events cause synchronised FPI outflows. Track DXY correlation.

## Rupee (INR/USD) and Equity Correlation

| Rupee Movement | Equity Impact |
|----------------|--------------|
| Rupee weakens (USD/INR rises) | IT sector benefits (export revenues); auto OEMs hurt (imported components) |
| Rupee strengthens | IT sector headwind; import-heavy sectors (pharma APIs, electronics) benefit |
| Sharp depreciation >1% in a session | FPI outflows risk → Nifty negative |
| Rupee at all-time weak level | RBI intervention likely; watch for rate action |

INR typically trades in a 1–2 rupee annual range in stable years. Sudden 5%+ moves signal macro stress and require defensive portfolio positioning.
