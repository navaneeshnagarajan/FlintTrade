# Product Modes

FlintTrade distinguishes operating modes from data provenance.

## Operating Modes

- **Explore**: Read-only discovery and visualisation.
- **Practice**: Simulated trading with sample data; no real orders or capital at risk.
- **Live**: Real trading with live market data and user-controlled authority.

## Provenance Labels

- **Sample**: Synthetic or historical sample data for illustration.
- **Unavailable**: Data source temporarily unavailable.
- **Live**: Real-time live data.
- **Stale**: Data that has not been refreshed within expected freshness window.

## Surface Ownership

- **Home**: Concise, read-only overview and discovery surface.
- **Trade**: Execution, charts, positions, orders, monitoring, and operational-risk controls.
