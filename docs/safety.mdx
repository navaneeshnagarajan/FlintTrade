# Safety

FlintTrade prioritises capital protection and user authority.

- All trading authority remains local and user-controlled.
- No LLM or documentation/MCP assistant receives independent Live-trading authority.
- Automated trading remains subject to explicit user authorisation and server-side safety controls.
- The kill switch, risk controls, and reconciliation remain enforced by the application and under user control.
- No documentation surface or MCP endpoint can alter or bypass them.
